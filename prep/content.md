## Part A: existing technology

##### Software
* Search: What apps do mechanical engineers use in the field?
* Site: https://onlinedegrees.sandiego.edu/best-engineering-software/
* Solidworks
  * Has a student plan
  * Is free to anyone
  * Compatible with a wide range of operating systems
  * [Solidworks](https://learnsolidworks.com/wp-content/uploads/solidworks-formula-1-course-600x400-1.jpg)
* Ansys
  *CAE/multiphysics engineering simulation software
  * Ansys for Students is free
  * Has multiple multiphysics Tools and Solvers
  * Has support for tons of different kinds of platforms.
  * [Ansys](https://ansys.synopsys.com/content/dam/news-center/a-drum-compressor-being-analyzed-with-ansys-mechanical.png)
* Wrike
  * A strong easy to use project management platform
  * free for individual task management
  * [Wrike](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/1*EzHb_PV4NvlKGyAwu6hhLQ.png)

##### Hardware
* Search: Mechanical engineering hardware used for prototyping
* Site: https://www.asme.org/topics-resources/content/10-tools-that-every-mechanical-engineer-should-know
* 3D printer
  * Easy to use
  * cheap material cost
  * Great for prototyping 
  * [3D printer](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/61ZR83H1unL.jpg)
* Coordinate measuring machines
  * Extremely accurate
  * Can do repetitive evaluation constantly
  * Efficient Labor Use
  * [Coordinate measuring machine](https://www.nolteprecise.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/12/Nolte_CMM-756x1024.jpg)
* Digital force gauge
  * Cheap and easy to use
  * Provides accurate data
  * Can do unit coversons
  * [Digital force gauge](https://www.sisco.com/images/uploaded/digital-force-gauge-details.jpg)
