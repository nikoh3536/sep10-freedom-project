# Content
Topic: **Mechanical Engineering**

## Overview/Introduction/Context
* Mechanical engineering has always been and will always be important in our lives, it is applied to our appliances cars and even small things like automatic doors.
* Mechanical engineers use tons of technology to help them envision problems solve tests and communicate their ideas so they can become a reality for the benefit of everybody.


## Categories

* Least complex
  * Basic everyday tools
  * [Wrike](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/1*EzHb_PV4NvlKGyAwu6hhLQ.png)
  * A simple user friendly application to communicate, track, and track work which is especially useful when working with a larger group of people on a project.
  * It also comes with a special discount for students, meaning they can get into the habit of using wrike so that when they need to in the future, they know how.
  * [Digital force gauge](https://www.sisco.com/images/uploaded/digital-force-gauge-details.jpg)
  * A simple easy to use but convenient tool for measuring tension or compression for any kind of prototype testing.
  * Simple to use and excellent for ensuring reliability for when the prototype is used in real world applications.
* Moderately complex
  * Beginner gadgets/software
  * [Solidworks](https://learnsolidworks.com/wp-content/uploads/solidworks-formula-1-course-600x400-1.jpg)
  * Excellent for solid modeling design, also renders mechanical parts seamlessly. Students can have access to it for free, making it accessible to anyone who wants to use it.
  * Quick to learn and easy to use, any student or dedicated engineer will love solidworks.
  * [3D printer](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/61ZR83H1unL.jpg)
  * Excellent for making, testing, or even visualizing prototypes, very easy to use and not ridiculously expensive.
  * Beginners and pros use a 3D printer all of the time when prototyping, it's not hard to learn and can even be fun.
* Most complex
  * Pros/industry grade.
  * [Ansys](https://ansys.synopsys.com/content/dam/news-center/a-drum-compressor-being-analyzed-with-ansys-mechanical.png)
  * Offers many kinds of professional tools such as 3D design, fluids simulations, structural analysis, electronic simulating/monetering, and optics simulation
  * Exells at prototype simulation in real world scenarios while offering ease of use and reduced complexity.
  * [Coordinate measuring machine](https://www.nolteprecise.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/12/Nolte_CMM-756x1024.jpg)
  * Extreme precision measurement ensures symmetry and any flaws will be found, improving prototype reliability.
  * Great for precision and getting a product ready for the real world.
